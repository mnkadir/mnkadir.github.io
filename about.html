Who I am

I am pursuing doctoral research in Geological Sciences with a specialization in hydrological science at the University of Florida.

I specialize in hydrodynamic modeling, flood risk management, and environmental resilience, with a focus on fluvial-deltaic systems and coastal regions. My work ranges from hydrological modeling and GIS applications to climate-change impacts, agricultural water management, sediment dynamics, and coupled ecohydraulic systems.

I lead the Impact of River Bed Changes on Estuary Water Levels study within the NSF-funded Megalopolitan Coastal Transformation Hub (MACH), collaborating across institutions to integrate geomorphic change into flood-modeling frameworks for the Delaware estuary and other vulnerable coastal systems.

What drives me is the opportunity to bridge rigorous science and actionable policy—advancing sustainable water management for communities facing rapid environmental change. Away from research, I am also a proud father.
