Research

Following signals through water & sediment.

Research spanning coastal geomorphology, hydrodynamics, ecohydraulics, agricultural water, and decision support—from process understanding to applied resilience.

01

MACH · MorphoSurge

Bathymetric change and estuarine flood risk

Effect of bathymetric changes on water-level variation in estuaries

Leading a research framework that develops and validates a moving-boundary approach to determine erosion and deposition under sea-level and sediment-supply variations, then connects evolving bathymetry to tropical and extratropical cyclone flood models.

  • Geomorphic laboratory experiments and numerical modeling
  • Sea-level, sediment-supply, river-discharge, and storm scenarios
  • NSF-funded Megalopolitan Coastal Transformation Hub collaboration

02

UF Watershed Ecology Lab

Flow–vegetation interactions

Coupled ecohydraulic modeling in river systems

Developing an integrated framework to quantify how submerged aquatic vegetation and river hydrodynamics shape one another, using observations and experiments alongside soft-body dynamics, finite-element structural solvers, and computational fluid dynamics.

03

UF Water Institute

Old Brahmaputra adaptive decision tool

Water management and river connectivity

Co-developed a stakeholder-informed framework combining hydrologic balance modeling, sediment management, and restoration options to improve dry-season flow, navigation, and ecological connectivity in a data-limited deltaic river.

Wageningen University & Research

Deltas under Pressure

Sustainable agricultural transitions in changing deltas

Contributed to interdisciplinary work on climate change, salinization, water quality, floods, droughts, and pathways for adapting agricultural production—with the broader aim of advancing Zero Hunger (SDG 2).

05

GBM Delta

Hydrodynamics, sediment & flood impacts

From Lower Meghna discharge to agricultural flood damage

Earlier work modeled flood scenarios, discharge, and flow volume in the Lower Meghna Estuary; examined sediment recirculation across the Ganges–Brahmaputra–Meghna delta; and connected flood simulations with crop and livelihood impacts.

Applied environmental research

Research for water-sensitive communities and agriculture

Projects include Buriganga River pollution and heavy-metal risk in rice systems, surface-water salinity intrusion in southwest Bangladesh, climate impacts on rice evapotranspiration, recycled wastewater irrigation, and community-based disaster preparedness.
